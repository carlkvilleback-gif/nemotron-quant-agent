Most trading ideas look amazing in a backtest. So I built an AI agent that proves them wrong.

Hi, I'm Carl Kvillebäck, an eighteen-year-old student from Sweden, and I built this together with my friends Isak Brosten and Leon Larsson.

This is Nemoo-tron Quant Researcher. You give it an idea, or it finds one in today's market news, and it does the whole research job on its own.

Let me show you. I click an example, pick my trading cost, and press run.

Under the hood, Nemoo-tron Super on Nebius Token Factory reads the news with Tavily and turns it into a testable rule. Then Nemoo-tron Ultra writes the backtest code. Before that code runs, a second agent reviews it. Here it caught a real bug: the strategy was holding six days instead of five. The coder fixed it, and the report shows what was caught.

The code runs in a sandbox without any API keys. If it crashes, or the numbers are impossible, the error goes back to Nemoo-tron, and it fixes its own code.

Now the honest part. Overnight trading in Micron returned over four thousand percent. But with point one percent cost per trade, almost three thousand trades turn that into one hundred and thirty-two percent. If you had simply held Micron, you would have made over two thousand percent. So the strategy doesn't really work in real-life scenarios.

And when an idea does beat the S and P five hundred, the agent checks if it was just luck. It re-tests both halves of the history, doubles the costs, and moves every number in the rule. Gold when the dollar falls looked great. After costs, it lost to the S and P, once again proving that the strategy would not work in real life.

Every idea lands on a leaderboard, ranked by how much it beat the S and P on the days it was actually invested. And the agent remembers what it tested, so it doesn't repeat itself.

Every weekday morning it runs completely by itself, and a full research cycle costs a few cents on Token Factory.

Most ideas come back weak. That's the point. Better to find out from an agent than from your bank account.

Have fun testing it! To protect my credits, the demo runs a maximum of ten times a day. It uses free daily prices from twenty fifteen, so no fundamentals like EV to EBIT. And remember, this is research, not financial advice.
